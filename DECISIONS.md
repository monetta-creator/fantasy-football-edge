# Decisions log

Every meaningful choice, newest at the bottom. Dates are absolute.

## 2026-09-05 — Phase 0 (draft assistant)

1. **Projection sources: ESPN + Sleeper (Rotowire), blended per raw stat.** Both verified live for the 2026 season on 2026-09-05. ESPN's `kona_player_info` gives raw stat projections for every position, projected games (stat id 210), ADP, and injury status. Sleeper's season projection endpoint gives Rotowire raw stats for QB/RB/WR/TE and PPR/half ADP. Blend weight 50/50 per stat; two independent projection sets cut single-source error. Sleeper's K projections omit sub-40-yard FGs and its DST projections have no points-allowed split, so K/DST use ESPN only. FantasyPros was checked and its ADP page markup changed; not used. nfl_data_py has no 2026 projections.
2. **Points are always computed from raw stats** (`backend/ffedge/scoring.py`), never imported. Unit tests cover the three hand-computed examples from the handoff plus kicker, DST negative bucket, and 2-pt cases.
3. **DST points-allowed buckets.** ESPN buckets 14-17 and 18-21 straddle Yahoo's 14-20 / 21-27 boundary; the 18-21 bucket is split 75/25. Also shipped a normal-approximation helper (sd 9.5) for sources that only give a mean.
4. **ADP consensus = 0.6 Yahoo average pick + 0.2 Sleeper PPR + 0.2 ESPN.** The league drafts in Yahoo's room, where casual drafters follow Yahoo's rank and average pick. Yahoo's public read-only API (`pub-api-ro.fantasysports.yahoo.com`) works without OAuth and also gives Yahoo status codes (IR, PUP-R, O, Q...) and bye weeks. Source ADP values above 300 are treated as undrafted.
5. **Pick variance model.** Each player's pick ~ Normal(ADP, sigma), sigma = 1 + 0.16·ADP combined with half the spread between ADP sources, capped at 35. An uncapped sigma let deep-bench players get "drafted" at pick 5 in simulation; that bug is what the cap fixes.
6. **Replacement level = expected best player left undrafted after 144 picks** (per position, from the ADP model), plus a streaming uplift of 0.5 ppg for K and 1.5 ppg for DEF. In a league with unlimited free acquisitions this is what a roster spot is really worth relative to. The lineup-allocation baseline (value over last starter, "VOLS") is computed too and shown as a secondary number. Empirical allocation across the pool: QB 12, RB 25, WR 35, TE 12 starters; flex slots went RB 13 / WR 11 / TE 0.
7. **Recommendations come from a vectorized Monte Carlo draft simulation**, not from VORP alone. Opponents pick by noisy ADP with positional caps (2 QB, 7 RB, 7 WR, 2 TE, 1 K, 1 DEF; no K/DEF before round 7). My future picks follow need-weighted VORP with forced K/DEF in the last two rounds. Each candidate at the current pick is scored by the mean value of my final roster (starters' VORP + 0.15 × bench VORP; IR-eligible players count their stash value and use no bench slot). 300 sims per candidate, common random numbers across candidates, ~1 s per recommendation.
8. **Confidence pill** = margin over the runner-up relative to its standard error: High (≥6 and >2 SE), Medium (≥2.5 and >1 SE), else Low.
9. **IR-stash value** = Σ over weeks from expected return through 17 of weight(w) × (ppg − replacement ppg) × return-risk factor, weights 0.5 (wk 1-7), 1.0 (8-14), 1.5 (15-17). Return week comes from ESPN's injuries feed `returnDate`; IR/PUP without a date defaults to week 5. Yahoo status determines IR-slot eligibility; "Out" counts only if `IR_PLUS=true`.
10. **LLM layer uses OpenRouter with open-source models, not the Anthropic API** (owner's instruction, cost). Default model `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` ($0.075/M in, $0.25/M out); `google/gemini-3.8-flash` and `qwen/qwen3.8-max-0902` also verified to exist on OpenRouter on 2026-09-05. The LLM only rewrites the rationale sentence in the background; the deterministic rationale is always shown first and numbers never come from the model.
11. **Draft picks are entered by tapping** in the UI. Live sync from Yahoo's draft room needs the OAuth app (Phase 1). Picks are sequential; the app knows who is on the clock from the snake order.
12. **Stack:** FastAPI + SQLite (`data/ffedge.db`) + numpy; Next.js 16 + Tailwind 4 with a `/api/*` rewrite to the backend so a phone only needs one host:port on the LAN. PWA manifest included; HTTPS is required for a real install, so on draft day it runs as a Safari "Add to Home Screen" bookmark.
13. **Season facts pulled from data:** 2026 has 18 regular-season weeks / 17 games (nflverse `games.csv`), fantasy playoffs weeks 15-17 per league settings. Sleeper's `gp` field is a placeholder (18 for everyone) and is ignored; ESPN's projected games are used.
14. **Source disagreement is surfaced** as `proj_spread` (points gap between ESPN and Sleeper under league scoring) and feeds nothing numeric yet; it is shown in the UI as an uncertainty hint.
15. **The app is a draft companion, not a draft client.** The owner drafts in the Yahoo app; "record pick" buttons only update the local board. Yahoo's write API (add/drop, lineup, IR moves) is reserved for Phase 1-2 behind the OAuth app and the two-week manual-approval guardrail.
16. **Draft-board screenshot import.** A photo/screenshot of the Yahoo board goes to an OpenRouter vision model (`OPENROUTER_VISION_MODEL`, default `google/gemini-3.8-flash`; `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` also accepts images) under a strict JSON schema that only allows transcription fields (pick number, round, column, name as written, position, NFL team, column header). Every transcribed name is fuzzy-matched to the player pool (initial+last-name aware, position/team disambiguation); rows are marked ok / ambiguous / unknown / already-drafted and the owner confirms before picks are applied in order. The model never chooses players and never sees our projections.
17. **LLM grounding check.** Any LLM-written rationale containing a number not present in the fact sheet, or a player name not among the candidates, is discarded (`backend/ffedge/llm.py::grounded`, unit-tested). This is the "forced call" rule: the model only rephrases numbers we computed.

## 2026-09-05 — Phase 1 (core loop, screenshot/manual sync path)

21. **Weekly projections come from Sleeper's weekly endpoint** (Rotowire raw stats per week, with opponent), scored with league rules. Sleeper omits the 50+ FG bucket, so it is derived as total FGs minus the listed buckets. DST points-allowed is a blend of Sleeper's `pts_allow` mean and the Vegas opponent implied total (50/50), converted to Yahoo's buckets with the normal approximation (sd 9.5). Kicker FG/XP expectations are scaled by the square root of the team's implied total relative to a 22.5-point league average. ESPN weekly projections were not wired: the endpoint returned season splits in testing and Sleeper's weekly data is complete.
22. **Vegas lines come from the nflverse schedule file** (closing spread and total per game), verified present for 2026 week 1. Implied team total = total/2 ± spread/2. No paid odds API.
23. **Weekly variance model** from nflverse 2025 weekly stats: per-player sd for anyone with ≥6 games, shrunk 70/30 toward position medians (CV: QB 0.47, RB 0.57, WR 0.58, TE 0.63, K 0.5, DEF 0.7). Weekly points are simulated as truncated normals.
24. **Lineup optimizer maximizes win probability, not projected points.** Candidates = the mean-optimal lineup plus every one-player swap with a bench alternate; each is simulated 20,000 times against the opponent's projected lineup. Maximizing P(win) reproduces the handoff's rule (variance when trailing, floor when favored) without hand-coding it. Opponent lineups use their recorded roster's mean-optimal lineup.
25. **Roster store** (`roster` table) holds all 12 teams. It is seeded from the draft board, then kept current by recorded moves (add/drop/move/IR), roster or transaction screenshots (vision transcription + name matching + confirmation), or the Yahoo client once approved. Every move is a *record* of what the owner did in Yahoo; nothing writes to Yahoo (the API is read-only anyway).
26. **Yahoo client is written and dormant**: OAuth2 code flow with `oob` redirect, token refresh, backoff on 429/5xx, and parsers for rosters, players, transactions, draft results, with fixture tests. Activates when `YAHOO_CLIENT_ID/SECRET` appear in `.env`.
27. **Scheduler**: APScheduler in-process refreshes the season pool every 6 h and weekly projections every 3 h. Running "with the laptop closed" needs a host; not done yet (candidate: a $5 VPS or a free-tier container). Logged as an open item.
28. **Recommendation cards on the dashboard** are generated deterministically: lineup change (Δ win probability), K/DEF stream when the best available beats mine by ≥1.5 projected points, IR move for any IR-eligible player not on IR, and bye-week starters. Each has a mechanism sentence and a confidence pill.

## 2026-09-05 — tool mode, About page, LLM made visible

29. **Text LLM default switched to `google/gemini-3.8-flash` with `reasoning.effort=minimal`.** A latency bake-off on OpenRouter with the strict JSON schema: GLM-5.3-flash cannot disable reasoning ("Reasoning is mandatory for this endpoint") and took 40-95 s per one-sentence rationale; Gemini 3.8 Flash answered in ~3 s (about 80 output tokens, well under a cent); Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite 0.8 s; Llama-4 Scout 3.4 s; Llama 3.3 70B overran the token budget. Model, vision model and reasoning effort are all `.env` settings.
30. **LLM status is reported, not hidden.** `llm.LAST` records the outcome of the latest call (ok / rejected by the grounding check / error / off) with model and latency; the recommendation card says whether the sentence is AI-written or deterministic text and why, and Settings shows the last call. Two on-demand grounded features were added: "Explain this pick" (fact sheet = recommended pick, top alternatives, drop-offs, confidence) and a per-player "AI summary" (projection, sources, 2025 results, week projection, injury note). Both use the same number-and-name grounding check (max 110 words).
31. **Draft screen is a tool, not a live monitor, by default.** No polling and no clock unless "Live" is toggled. A Rankings tab shows the full board with VORP tiers (a tier breaks when VORP drops by more than max(6, 6% of the top)) and one-tap "Gone" / "Mine" marks; the pick number is inferred from how many players are marked, so team attribution is optional.
32. **About page** documents every metric, its formula and its data source.

## 2026-09-05 — Decide flow and player analytics

33. **Decide tab (default on the draft screen).** One button runs the recommender on the marked board and returns the top two candidates by simulated roster value, each with a deterministic reason list: value vs replacement and vs the next player at the position, scarcity at my next pick (and whether it is the steepest drop-off), availability at my next pick, roster fit (which slot he fills given what I have), the simulation margin and confidence, the scoring mechanism, market vs model rank among available players, injury/games/bye-overlap/source-disagreement risks, and 2025 per-game average with volatility. An optional grounded AI comparison of A vs B. The owner chooses; "I took …" records the pick for my team. Opponent picks are attributed via the team picker, which is now always visible on the mark sheet.
34. **LLM name guard relaxed to token overlap.** The first version flagged "Draft Christian McCaffrey" as an unknown name because of the sentence-initial verb. A capitalised phrase now passes if it contains any token of an allowed name; invented players are still rejected. Rejections report the exact offending number or name.
35. **Player analytics** on the detail page: 2025 per-game rates (targets, catch %, yards/target, carries, yards/carry, touches, TD/game; QB attempt, completion %, yards/attempt, TD and INT rates; K FG %, 50+; DST sacks, takeaways, points allowed), consistency (startable %, boom ≥25, bust <8, median, last-4 average, first vs second half trend), a 3-week rolling average on the chart, position ranks (2026 projection, VORP, ADP, 2025 ppg), and a benchmark: search any player to overlay his 2025 line and compare every metric side by side. Startable thresholds: QB 18, RB/WR 12, TE 9, K 8, DEF 7.
36. **"Gone by #N" demoted.** It only informs a decision when low (you could wait on the player), so it left the Decide and recommendation hero tiles in favor of **cost of waiting** (drop-off at the position to my next pick) and stays in the Rankings list and the availability reason.
37. **Advanced 2025 metrics** from nflverse weekly stats: target share, air-yards share, rush share (player carries / team carries from the team weekly file), WOPR, aDOT, RACR, YAC per reception, first downs per game, and EPA (per game, per target, per carry, per dropback). Red-zone touches are not in the weekly files (need play-by-play) and were skipped.

## 2026-09-05 — market data (Vegas, props, Kalshi) and correlated simulation

38. **Vegas consistency check and scaling.** Per NFL team, points implied by our player projections (6 × TDs + 3 × FGs + XPs; receiving TDs are the passing TDs) are compared with the Vegas implied total. Skill players on a team are scaled by (implied/projected)^0.5, clipped to 0.85–1.15. The table is shown on the Week tab with flags for teams outside ±15%.
39. **Correlated game-level Monte Carlo replaces independent draws.** Each game's total and margin are drawn from the line (sd 13.5 each), team points follow, and every player's mean scales with his team's simulated score (exponent 0.75 for QB/WR, 0.7 TE, 0.55 RB, 0.8 K) plus residual noise sized so total variance is roughly unchanged. DST draws use the opponent's simulated points for the points-allowed bucket and scale sacks/turnovers against the opponent's scoring. Lineup win probability, floors and ceilings now reflect stacks. Unit test asserts QB–WR correlation > 0.2 and QB–opposing DST < −0.1.
40. **Sportsbook props via The Odds API** (owner's free key). Six markets per game (pass yds, pass TDs, rush yds, receptions, rec yds, anytime TD) from US books, lines averaged across books, prices de-vigged; a line is treated as the median and shifted by the over/under lean (normal quantile × a per-stat sd fraction); anytime-TD probability becomes expected TDs via −ln(1−p). Props are converted to league points for the components they cover and blended 50/50 into the weekly projection; the gap is shown as market − model. **Credits are spent only on an explicit pull** (button or the weekly job); page loads read the cached pull. One pull ≈ 90 credits (15 games × 6 markets); the free tier is 500/month, so plan one pull per week. The first automatic load spent 90 credits before that rule existed.
41. **Kalshi prediction market** (public API, no key): game winners, total/spread/team-total ladders, anytime TD, player yardage and fantasy-point ladders, season win totals. Ladders are reduced to implied medians/means. Markets were unpriced five days before week 1; the module tolerates that and fills in as liquidity arrives.
42. **Every market surface carries an ⓘ sticker with interpretive notes and a ✨ button** that asks the model to explain the block from a server-built fact sheet (topics: market_week, consistency, matchup, kalshi, player_market). The grounding check now also accepts city and source names, determiner-led phrases, and absolute values of negative-looking numbers such as "10th-90th".
