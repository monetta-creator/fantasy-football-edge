# Decisions log

Every meaningful choice, newest at the bottom. Dates are absolute.

## 2026-09-05 — Phase 0 (draft assistant)

1. **Projection sources: ESPN + Sleeper (Rotowire), blended per raw stat.** Both verified live for the 2026 season on 2026-09-05. ESPN's `kona_player_info` gives raw stat projections for every position, projected games (stat id 210), ADP, and injury status. Sleeper's season projection endpoint gives Rotowire raw stats for QB/RB/WR/TE and PPR/half ADP. Blend weight 50/50 per stat; two independent projection sets cut single-source error. Sleeper's K projections omit sub-40-yard FGs and its DST projections have no points-allowed split, so K/DST use ESPN only. FantasyPros was checked and its ADP page markup changed; not used. nfl_data_py has no 2026 projections.
2. **Points are always computed from raw stats** (`backend/ffedge/scoring.py`), never imported. Unit tests cover the three hand-computed examples from the handoff plus kicker, DST negative bucket, and 2-pt cases.
3. **DST points-allowed buckets.** ESPN buckets 14-17 and 18-21 straddle Yahoo's 14-20 / 21-27 boundary; the 18-21 bucket is split 75/25. Also shipped a normal-approximation helper (sd 9.5) for sources that only give a mean.
4. **ADP consensus = 0.6 Yahoo average pick + 0.2 Sleeper PPR + 0.2 ESPN.** The league drafts in Yahoo's room, where casual drafters follow Yahoo's rank and average pick. Yahoo's public read-only API (`pub-api-ro.fantasysports.yahoo.com`) works without OAuth and also gives Yahoo status codes (IR, PUP-R, O, Q...) and bye weeks. Source ADP values above 300 are treated as undrafted.
5. **Pick variance model.** Each player's pick ~ Normal(ADP, sigma), sigma = 1 + 0.16·ADP combined with half the spread between ADP sources, capped at 35. An uncapped sigma let deep-bench players get "drafted" at pick 5 in simulation; that bug is what the cap fixes.
6. **Replacement level = expected best player left undrafted after 144 picks** (per position, from the ADP model), plus a streaming uplift of 0.5 ppg for K and 1.5 ppg for DEF. In a league with unlimited free acquisitions this is what a roster spot is really worth relative to. The lineup-allocation baseline (value over last starter, "VOLS") is computed too and shown as a secondary number. Empirical allocation across the pool: QB 12, RB 25, WR 35, TE 12 starters; flex slots went RB 13 / WR 11 / TE 0.
7. **Recommendations come from a vectorized Monte Carlo draft simulation**, not from VORP alone. Opponents pick by noisy ADP with positional caps (2 QB, 7 RB, 7 WR, 2 TE, 1 K, 1 DEF; no K/DEF before round 7). My future picks follow need-weighted VORP with forced K/DEF in the last two rounds. Each candidate at the current pick is scored by the mean value of my final roster (starters' VORP + 0.15 × bench VORP; IR-eligible players count their stash value and use no bench slot). 300 sims per candidate, common random numbers across candidates, ~1 s per recommendation.
8. **Confidence pill** = margin over the runner-up relative to its standard error: High (≥6 and >2 SE), Medium (≥2.5 and >1 SE), else Low.
9. **IR-stash value** = Σ over weeks from expected return through 17 of weight(w) × (ppg − replacement ppg) × return-risk factor, weights 0.5 (wk 1-7), 1.0 (8-14), 1.5 (15-17). Return week comes from ESPN's injuries feed `returnDate`; IR/PUP without a date defaults to week 5. Yahoo status determines IR-slot eligibility; "Out" counts only if `IR_PLUS=true`.
10. **LLM layer uses OpenRouter with open-source models, not the Anthropic API** (owner's instruction, cost). Default model `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` ($0.075/M in, $0.25/M out); `google/gemini-3.8-flash` and `qwen/qwen3.8-max-0902` also verified to exist on OpenRouter on 2026-09-05. The LLM only rewrites the rationale sentence in the background; the deterministic rationale is always shown first and numbers never come from the model.
11. **Draft picks are entered by tapping** in the UI. Live sync from Yahoo's draft room needs the OAuth app (Phase 1). Picks are sequential; the app knows who is on the clock from the snake order.
12. **Stack:** FastAPI + SQLite (`data/ffedge.db`) + numpy; Next.js 16 + Tailwind 4 with a `/api/*` rewrite to the backend so a phone only needs one host:port on the LAN. PWA manifest included; HTTPS is required for a real install, so on draft day it runs as a Safari "Add to Home Screen" bookmark.
13. **Season facts pulled from data:** 2026 has 18 regular-season weeks / 17 games (nflverse `games.csv`), fantasy playoffs weeks 15-17 per league settings. Sleeper's `gp` field is a placeholder (18 for everyone) and is ignored; ESPN's projected games are used.
14. **Source disagreement is surfaced** as `proj_spread` (points gap between ESPN and Sleeper under league scoring) and feeds nothing numeric yet; it is shown in the UI as an uncertainty hint.
15. **The app is a draft companion, not a draft client.** The owner drafts in the Yahoo app; "record pick" buttons only update the local board. Yahoo's write API (add/drop, lineup, IR moves) is reserved for Phase 1-2 behind the OAuth app and the two-week manual-approval guardrail.
16. **Draft-board screenshot import.** A photo/screenshot of the Yahoo board goes to an OpenRouter vision model (`OPENROUTER_VISION_MODEL`, default `google/gemini-3.8-flash`; `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` also accepts images) under a strict JSON schema that only allows transcription fields (pick number, round, column, name as written, position, NFL team, column header). Every transcribed name is fuzzy-matched to the player pool (initial+last-name aware, position/team disambiguation); rows are marked ok / ambiguous / unknown / already-drafted and the owner confirms before picks are applied in order. The model never chooses players and never sees our projections.
17. **LLM grounding check.** Any LLM-written rationale containing a number not present in the fact sheet, or a player name not among the candidates, is discarded (`backend/ffedge/llm.py::grounded`, unit-tested). This is the "forced call" rule: the model only rephrases numbers we computed.
